Making a visual

FYI: I had Claude create this page for me based on the session history from a long conversation on decision models. The lead/summary for each section is generated. This page basically just exists to illustrate what the process can look like.

This page shows every iteration behind the two interactive visuals in Understanding Jev and System 1 Models, with each round moving from my feedback to AI-built candidates and another review.

6Transport controls

This round made every button solid, extended hover replay to every word, replaced waves with sparkles in the weights, and exchanged the word-labelled buttons for a transport bar, with the bolder version becoming the first finished visual.

7A second visual: side by side

A voice note proposed putting the same question to a coaxed LLM and a decision model: the LLM types JSON one character per pass through the weights, while the decision model fires once and stamps the complete answer.

8Five polish turns

Focused edits made the inputs directly comparable, paired corresponding data on hover, kept each row to one line with probabilities inside the JSON, let the LLM drift into a red conversational coda, and revised the transport and probability controls before the visual shown in step 7 was embedded in the article.

The prose in my articles is handwritten, while the visuals come from a collaboration in which I describe the idea, AI builds candidates, and I review and correct them. I explain the wider process in How I Use AI in Writing.
